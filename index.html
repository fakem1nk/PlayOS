PlayOS

A free, open-source game launcher for Windows that turns your PC into a game console: a PS5-style interface you control with a gamepad from the couch.

DownloadSource codePrivacy Policy

What PlayOS does

  • Launches PC games (Steam and non-Steam), PlayStation 2 games via the PCSX2 emulator and PlayStation 3 games via the RPCS3 emulator from one console-style library.
  • Works entirely with a gamepad (DualSense, DualShock 4, Xbox and others): navigation, an in-game overlay, quick saves for emulators.
  • Tracks playtime and play sessions, shows trophies (Steam and RetroAchievements), game descriptions and cover art.
  • Optionally syncs game saves, your profile and your game library between your own PCs through your own cloud storage.

How PlayOS uses Google Drive

Google Drive access is optional and only used for cloud saves. When you choose “Sign in” with Google Drive in PlayOS settings, PlayOS requests the drive.file scope and creates a “PlayOS” folder in your Drive. It stores there only the files it creates itself: copies of your game saves, your PlayOS profile and your game list, so that another PC signed in to the same Google account can download them. PlayOS cannot see or change any other files in your Drive. PlayOS has no servers: data travels only between your PC and your own Google Drive, and the developer never receives it.

PlayOS по-русски

Бесплатный лаунчер игр для Windows с открытым кодом в стиле PS5: игры PC, PS2 (PCSX2) и PS3 (RPCS3) в одной библиотеке, управление геймпадом, время игры, трофеи и облачные сохранения.

Google Drive нужен только для облачных сохранений, по желанию: PlayOS создаёт папку «PlayOS» на вашем Диске и видит только свои файлы — сохранения, профиль и список игр. Своих серверов у PlayOS нет. Подробнее — в политике конфиденциальности.

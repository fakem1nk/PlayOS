PlayOS Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · PlayOS home page

PlayOS (“the app”) is a free, open-source game launcher for Windows developed by fakem1nk (github.com/fakem1nk/PlayOS). This policy explains what data PlayOS accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how you can delete it. PlayOS has no servers of its own: the developer does not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data.

1. Data stored on your computer

PlayOS stores your game library (game titles, file paths, cover art), playtime and play sessions, PlayOS trophies, settings and API keys you enter yourself only on your computer, in %APPDATA%\PlayOS. This data never leaves your computer unless you turn on cloud sync.

2. Google user data accessed by PlayOS

Cloud sync is optional. If you choose to sign in with Google Drive, PlayOS requests one OAuth scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — access only to files that PlayOS itself creates in your Google Drive. PlayOS cannot list, read, change or delete any other files in your Drive.

PlayOS also reads your Google account display name and your Drive storage usage (through the Drive about endpoint) to show which account is connected and how much space is left.

3. How the data is used

PlayOS uses Google Drive only to provide the cloud sync feature you turned on: it creates a “PlayOS” folder in your Drive and stores there copies of your game save files, your PlayOS profile (name, avatar, settings, trophies) and your game list (titles, playtime, sessions, art links), so that your other PCs signed in to the same Google account can download them. Your display name and storage usage are shown only inside the app.

4. Storage and security

Data travels directly between your computer and your own Google Drive over HTTPS. OAuth tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with the Windows Data Protection API. The developer has no copy of your tokens or your files.

5. Sharing

PlayOS does not share, sell, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone, including the developer. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and is not read by humans.

PlayOS’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Other services

You can also choose Yandex Disk (stored in “Apps/PlayOS”, app-folder access only) or a local sync folder (OneDrive, Dropbox and similar) instead of Google Drive. For cover art, game descriptions and trophies PlayOS contacts public services (Steam, SteamGridDB, Wikipedia, HowLongToBeat, RetroAchievements, the Ludusavi manifest on GitHub) without sending your personal data, except API keys you entered yourself for those services.

7. Retention and deletion

Files stay in your Drive until you delete them. To stop sync, choose “Sign out of the cloud” in PlayOS settings; PlayOS then deletes its stored tokens. You can revoke PlayOS access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the “PlayOS” folder from your Drive like any other folder. Uninstalling PlayOS and deleting %APPDATA%\PlayOS removes all local data.

8. Children

PlayOS does not knowingly collect data from anyone; it has no accounts or servers of its own.

9. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the project repository.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: GitHub Issues or the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.

Политика конфиденциальности PlayOS

Действует с 25 октября 2026 г.

PlayOS — бесплатный лаунчер игр для Windows с открытым кодом. У PlayOS нет своих серверов: разработчик не собирает, не получает и не хранит ваши данные.

На вашем ПК

Библиотека игр, время, трофеи, настройки и ключи API хранятся только в %APPDATA%\PlayOS.

Облачные сохранения (по желанию)

При входе в Google Drive PlayOS запрашивает только доступ drive.file — к файлам, которые создал сам, в папке «PlayOS» на вашем Диске: копии сохранений игр, профиль и список игр. Остальные файлы Диска PlayOS не видит. Также читаются имя аккаунта и занятое место — только для показа в приложении. Яндекс Диск — папка «Приложения/PlayOS». Данные передаются напрямую между ПК и вашим облаком, токены хранятся на ПК в зашифрованном виде. Данные Google не передаются третьим лицам, не используются для рекламы и обучения ИИ; использование соответствует Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use).

Удаление

«Выйти из облака» в настройках PlayOS удаляет токены. Доступ можно отозвать на myaccount.google.com/permissions или id.yandex.ru, папку PlayOS в облаке — удалить как обычную папку.

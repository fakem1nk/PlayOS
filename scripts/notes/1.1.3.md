# PlayOS v1.1.3

> **Update: Tivi Comes Alive**

---

## ✨ What's New

- Added new things for Tivi to do.
  - Tivi shows the update download progress right on his face.
  - In the morning Tivi stretches. After midnight he puts on a nightcap and yawns more often.
  - Tivi waves his antenna on the first launch of the day and is overjoyed when you come back after **3 days** or more.
  - Pet Tivi: hold the mouse button on him and move the cursor back and forth.
  - When left alone, Tivi sometimes plays a tiny game of pong with himself.
- Added a setting to turn Tivi off in **Settings → Display**.

---

## 📦 Installation

Download the appropriate file from the **Assets** section below.

If PlayOS is already installed, you can update directly through the application.

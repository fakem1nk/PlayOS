# PlayOS v1.1.0-beta.4

> **Update: Smoother Controls & Controller Vibration**

---

## ✨ What's New

- Added **Vibration in menus** (Settings → Controller and overlay, off by default): a light response at the end of a list, when a menu opens, and a build-up while you hold ✕ on a game that ends with a tap when the options open.
  - Works with Xbox controllers and DualSense, including when Steam or DSX present the DualSense as an Xbox controller.
- Holding a direction now speeds up smoothly instead of jumping in one step, and starts sooner.
- On the on-screen keyboard you can hold ▢ to keep erasing and hold L1/R1 to move the cursor.
- If you start typing on a physical keyboard while the on-screen keyboard is open, the text goes straight into the field.

---

## 🛠 Fixes

- The left stick no longer makes two steps at once when tilted diagonally, and no longer jitters near the edge of its range.
- After closing a dialog or menu, focus returns to the item you were on instead of jumping to the first one.
- Keys like C, F or Backspace on a physical keyboard no longer close or edit the on-screen keyboard by accident.
- Tab no longer moves focus to buttons hidden behind an open window.
- Holding Esc or Backspace no longer closes several windows in a row.
- The controller feels the same when PlayOS is open on top of a game.

---

## 📦 Installation

Download the appropriate file from the **Assets** section below.

If PlayOS is already installed, you can update directly through the application.

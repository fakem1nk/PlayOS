# PlayOS v1.1.1

> **Patch: Notification Settings & Tray Icon Fix**

---

## ✨ What's New

- Added notification settings right in the Notification Center: press the gear button or **Triangle**.
  - Turn pop-ups off, and notifications quietly collect in the center. Errors and responses to your actions still show.
  - Turn trophy pop-ups on or off.
  - Choose the notification size: **Small**, **Normal** or **Large**.
  - Choose how long notifications stay on screen.
- The Notification Center now keeps only what matters: errors, trophies and updates.
- Improved the window frame: a visible title bar shows where to drag the window, and the top bar sits a little further from it.

## 🛠️ Fixed

- Fixed the tray icon sometimes showing up blank, especially when PlayOS starts with Windows.
- Fixed the window frame missing when PlayOS starts in a window instead of full screen.

## 🗑️ Removed

- Removed the session summary after closing a game: play time is on the game page and in your profile.
- Removed confirmations for changes you can already see, such as a new avatar, a new name or saved game edits.

---

## 📦 Installation

Download the appropriate file from the **Assets** section below.

If PlayOS is already installed, you can update directly through the application.

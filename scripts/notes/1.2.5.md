# PlayOS v1.2.5

> **Update: Holidays, Tivi Figures & New Sounds**

---

## ✨ What's New

- Added holiday decorations for **New Year**, **Halloween** and **Valentine's Day**.
  - New Year: snow, a string of lights, a snowdrift, fireworks and a comet.
  - Halloween: a moon, bats, a spider on a thread, a cobweb, glowing pumpkins and a ghost.
  - Valentine's Day: floating hearts.
  - Each holiday has its own interface sounds and its own intro sound.
  - Decorations turn on by the calendar; you can switch them off or pick a holiday yourself in the settings.
- Added seasonal moods for Tivi: he catches snowflakes and shivers in winter, grows a flower in spring, melts in summer and watches leaves fall in autumn.
  - On holidays he wears a hat, decorates a tree, launches fireworks, carves a pumpkin, meets a ghost and gets a love letter.
- Added new things Tivi does when left alone: he lights a fireplace on his screen, watches an aquarium, plays snake, dances, sneezes, plays hide-and-seek and more.
- Added **7** new Tivi figures to the profile, **14** in total.
  - New figures are earned for finishing a game in three days, at night, with **100** hours played, for **100%** completions and more.
  - Every figure now has its own look and its own animated scene when you open it.
  - Figures have a rarity, and locked ones show how much is left.
- Redesigned the interface sounds: they are softer and rounder now.
- Redesigned the background music to match the new sounds.
- Redesigned how Tivi appears at the end of the intro: the symbols of the name fly into the corner, build him out of characters and a scan line turns them into Tivi.
- Redesigned the README.

## 🛠️ Fixed

- Fixed the home screen flashing for a moment before the intro.
- Fixed the intro sound drifting away from the picture.
- Fixed a faint click at the start of some interface sounds.

# PlayOS v1.2.0-beta.3

> **Major Update: Built-in Downloads, Unpacking & Install**

---

## ✨ What's New

- Added downloads right inside PlayOS: press **Download** on a game and pick a variant from your sources.
  - Direct links resume after a pause or a restart and try the next mirror when one fails.
  - Torrent and magnet links download through PlayOS's own client, with no seeding afterwards.
- Added the **Downloads** section: open it with the new button in the top bar, which shows the progress as a ring.
  - The current download is shown big, with speed and time left.
  - Pause, resume, move up in the queue, open the folder or cancel right from each row, no extra menus.
- Added choosing where each game is downloaded: pick one of your folders by free space or create a new one in one press.
- Added automatic unpacking of downloaded archives: zip, 7z, rar and archives split into parts.
  - PlayOS checks free space first and asks for the archive password only when it is needed.
  - Archives are deleted after a successful unpack; you can keep them in Settings → Store.
- Added installing games after the download:
  - Ready-to-play games, PS2 images and PS3 games go to your library on their own.
  - Games with an installer get an **Install** button, which then turns into **Play**.
- Added short labels in the download variants: who made the release, version, DLC and size.
- Added the finish time to finished downloads; they leave the list on their own after 3 days.

## 🛠️ Fixed

- Fixed games added from the store showing up in **Recently played** before you played them.
- Fixed pressing a game that is not installed in the Library opening the download right away.

---

## 📦 Installation

Download the appropriate file from the **Assets** section below.

If PlayOS is already installed, you can update directly through the application.
